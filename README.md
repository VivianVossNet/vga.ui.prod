# vga.ui

The Video Game Almanach's interface for **World of Warcraft: Forever** - one calm, consistent look
for the whole screen, drawn with its own graphics library, LibVGA.

**Version 0.9.17 - beta.** A version for testing. Settings, places and looks may still change.

## What it brings

- Player, target and pet frames, the target's pet and the target of target, the party frame, cast bars, buffs and nameplates in one style; every frame a target for spells, a dead unit's portrait grey, the leader's crown; buffs at the frames or gathered at the top of the screen
- Eight action bars of its own, in one row, two rows or freely placed
- The experience bar with rested bonus, reputation, system values, money and bag slots
- One bag window with search, filters, sorting, tidying and special bags set apart; the loot as a dialog in the same look; items you cannot use veiled in red, upgrades marked with a green dot
- A minimap in four by three or square, three sizes, with zone, coordinates, time, a compass rose
  and every other addon's minimap button gathered beneath it
- A quest tracker with navigation to the nearest or the chosen quest
- A small damage meter from the game's own figures: damage, healing, taken, deaths and more, per fight or overall, your own row always in view
- Arcs beside your character for range, threat, the target's cast, health or your resource, shown in combat only if you wish
- vga.auctions, an auction window of its own: buy with filters by kind, quality and level, sell with a price history, your own auctions with the undercut ones marked, holdings, a ledger of every purchase and sale, and items to watch; market values from your own readings of the auction house
- Arranging every element on a free or snapped grid; layouts per character and as named profiles
- Texts in German, English, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese and Russian

## Installing

Through [Tinkerbox](https://vga.run/en/tinkerbox), or by hand: copy the two folders `LibVGA` and
`vga.ui` into `World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns` and restart the game.

Open the settings with `/vga`, from the VGA badge beneath the minimap, or under AddOns in the game's
settings.
